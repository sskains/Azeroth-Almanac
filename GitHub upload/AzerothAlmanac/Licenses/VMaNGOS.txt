Azeroth Almanac - Data\Creatures.lua

The creature data in Data\Creatures.lua (levels, Classic health / armor / damage estimates,
ability spell IDs, immunities, resistances, loot and skinning tables with drop chances, money
ranges) is derived from the VMaNGOS world database:

  VMaNGOS - https://github.com/vmangos/core
  Database snapshot "db_latest" (release db-sqlite-4641790), Classic patch 1.12 state.

Thank you to the VMaNGOS contributors, and to the Elysium / LightsHope projects before them.

It was converted by tools\build_creatures.py. Only IDs and numbers are included; names,
icons and descriptions are supplied by the game client.
